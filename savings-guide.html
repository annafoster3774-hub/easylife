How to Set a Savings Goal

A savings goal becomes easier to manage when you turn one large number into a small, repeatable monthly target.

1. Choose one clear goal

Decide what you are saving for and write down the amount you want to reach. A specific target makes progress easier to see.

2. Subtract what you already have

If you have already saved part of the amount, count it toward the goal. This shows the real gap that remains.

3. Choose a monthly contribution

Pick an amount that is realistic for your budget. Consistency matters more than choosing an amount that is difficult to maintain.

4. Track the number of months

Divide the remaining amount by your monthly contribution and round up. This gives you a simple estimate of how long the goal may take.
