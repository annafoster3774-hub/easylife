How to Plan Meals for a Week

Weekly meal planning can be as simple as deciding what you will eat before you go shopping. The goal is not perfection — it is fewer decisions during a busy week.

1. Choose seven simple meal ideas

Pick meals you already know how to make. Mix familiar favourites with one or two new ideas if you want variety.

2. Check what you already have

Look through your fridge, freezer and cupboards before making a shopping list. Use ingredients you already own where possible.

3. Build a focused shopping list

Write down only what you need for the planned meals and a few basic staples. This can make shopping quicker and reduce impulse purchases.

4. Leave room for flexibility

You do not have to follow the plan perfectly. Move meals between days when your schedule changes and use leftovers when they make sense.
