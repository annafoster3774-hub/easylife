How to Start a Budget

A budget does not need to be complicated. Start with a clear picture of your monthly income, your essential costs and the amount you want to keep for savings or flexible spending.

1. Write down your monthly income

Use the amount you normally receive each month. If your income changes, use a realistic average rather than your best month.

2. List your main expenses

Start with housing, groceries, transport and other regular costs. You can add more categories later if you need them.

3. Calculate what is left

Subtract your total expenses from your income. This gives you a simple starting point for deciding how much you can save or spend on flexible items.

4. Keep the system simple

Check your budget once a week and adjust it when something changes. A simple budget you actually use is more useful than a complicated one you abandon.
